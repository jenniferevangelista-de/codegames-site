Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

The CodeGames app is a private tool owned by the CodeGames YouTube channel. It connects only to the channel's own YouTube account, using the YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos, with title, description, thumbnail and publish time.
  • Replies to comments on the channel's own videos.

Data

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other person. It does not use cookies or analytics on this site. The only credentials it uses are the channel owner's own authorization token, kept as a private secret in the channel's private code repository. Comment text is read only to decide whether to post a short reply, and is not saved.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
